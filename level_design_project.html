As part of an assignment for a level design course that I was taking, I created a level in Unreal Engine, following an iterative design process.

The level was playtested weekly, and I targeted my efforts at addressing feedback that I had recieved.

The Initial Concept

I wanted to make a level set in a morally bankrupt technology company of sorts. The goal of the player was to break in, retrieve some secretive data, and escape, all without being caught.

The theme was to be explicitly corporate, and the player is meant to feel like they were breaking into an office environment.

The Development Process

The first task at hand was to create a blockout of the level so that I could see how players would experience moving through the level on a larger scale.

My focus was not on making engaging content at this stage, I was just trying to ensure that the transitions players take as they move through different phases of the level felt natural and satisfying.
